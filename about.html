About Me

Background

I am from Imo State, Nigeria. My professional path began in the hospitality and food industry, where I took on increasing responsibility as Head Chef and later as Executive Chef. Those roles taught me how to manage people, maintain standards under pressure, and turn ideas into plates that guests actually enjoy.

Outside the kitchen I developed a strong interest in photography, editing, and digital media. That interest grew into SleekCore Photos, a creative outlet where I work with images and help others who want to learn the craft.

Education & Current Focus

I am currently studying cybersecurity. The decision came from a practical place: so much of modern life sits online, and the systems that hold our data, money, and conversations need people who understand how to protect them. I am building the technical foundation now so I can contribute meaningfully later.

My longer-term goal includes further study and international exposure. I see continuous learning as the most reliable way to stay useful and competitive.

Career Aspirations

I want to build businesses that last — starting with food and hospitality, then expanding into education and media. At the same time I intend to grow as a technology professional, especially in areas that keep digital systems safe and trustworthy. The common thread is the same: do good work, create opportunities for others, and leave something solid behind.

Technical Skills

Kitchen Operations Menu Development Team Leadership Photography Photo & Video Editing Cybersecurity Foundations Digital Media

Hobbies & Interests

Beyond work I enjoy photography, exploring new dishes, following developments in technology and security, and spending time on projects that combine creativity with practical outcomes. I value independence, steady personal growth, and the freedom that comes from building something of my own.
